<p class="eyebrow">About</p>
<h1>The public layer of Studio Lab.</h1>
<p class="lede">This site is an outward-facing index of selected research, projects, writing, and methods. It is deliberately separate from the private dashboard used to monitor work in progress.</p>

<div class="prose">
## Two different dashboards

The internal dashboard exists to answer operational questions: what is running, what failed, what needs review, and what should happen next. The public site answers a different question: what finished work is useful for someone outside the system to inspect, try, or read?

The public layer therefore does not mirror internal state automatically.

## Publishing principles

Research pages should make it possible to tell what was directly observed, what is interpretation, what remains uncertain, and what was not measured. Projects should link only to public deployments that can be verified. Writing should point to published articles rather than drafts or editorial queues.

## What is not published automatically

Internal working documents, credentials, private data, operational logs, live work queues, and unpublished claims are not copied to this site by default. Adding or materially changing a substantive public claim should go through review before it is merged into the publishing branch.

## Source and history

The website source and revision history are maintained in GitHub. Research evidence and editorial records may originate elsewhere; the public site is a presentation layer, not a replacement for the underlying source of truth.
