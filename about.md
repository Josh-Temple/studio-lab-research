<p class="eyebrow">About</p>
<h1>What this site is for</h1>
<p class="lede">Studio Lab Research is the public-facing layer for selected research outputs. It is not the canonical store for internal research state or unpublished evidence.</p>

<div class="prose">
## Publishing principles

Research pages should make it possible to tell what was directly observed, what is interpretation, what remains uncertain, and what was not measured.

A public page should normally include the research question, method, observations or results, limitations, and links to supporting material that can be shared safely.

## What is not published automatically

Internal working documents, credentials, private data, operational logs, and unpublished claims are not copied to this site by default. Adding or materially changing a public research page should go through review before it is merged into the publishing branch.

## Source and history

The website source and its revision history are maintained in GitHub. Research evidence may originate elsewhere; a research page should identify its public evidence sources without treating the website itself as the original evidence store.
